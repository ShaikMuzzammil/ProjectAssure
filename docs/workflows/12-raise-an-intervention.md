# Workflow 12 · Raise an intervention

**One line:** Convert any issue into a tracked 7-step intervention — detected to closed, with owners, deadlines and evidence.

**Who uses it:** whoever is accountable for actually fixing the issue.

## Click path
1. From an alert, a recommended action, or the **Interventions Centre** (⌘K) → **Raise intervention**.
2. Fill the issue, severity, owner and first corrective step.
3. Advance it through the lifecycle, adding updates and evidence as you go.

## The 7-step lifecycle
**Detected → Reviewed → Action Assigned → Under Investigation → Resolved → Verified → Closed**

Each step records who, when and what changed — the full timeline is on every intervention card.

## What you get
- A decision queue: every live intervention with its current step (e.g. "3/7").
- Corrective-step checklists and evidence counts.
- **The rule that makes it governance:** the intelligence engine only advises — people decide, verify and close.

## Verify it worked
Raise an intervention from Bharatmala's top recommended action → it appears at step 1 with you as raiser; advance it two steps → the timeline shows both updates with timestamps.

## Under the hood (honesty)
Interventions are store-backed with full audit linkage; exports include lifecycle position (workflow 13).
