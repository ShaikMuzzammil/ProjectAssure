# Workflow 09 · Get recommended actions

**One line:** Open the plan of action: ranked what-to-do steps with owner, deadline, expected impact — and the cost of doing nothing.

**Who uses it:** the officer who has to decide *what to do on Monday morning*.

## Click path
1. Open any project → **Plan of Action** tab.
2. Read the ranked actions (P1 → P3): **what is happening → why it matters → what to do → owner → deadline → expected impact**.
3. Scroll to **"What happens if we do nothing"** — the 30/60/90-day projection.
4. Open the **root-cause tree** (schedule / resources / approvals, with contribution weights).

## What you get
- Actions ranked by impact, each convertible to a **tracked intervention** in one click (workflow 12).
- The no-action projection: risk level and cost trajectory if nobody acts.
- KPI watch list for this project.

## The project-scoped version (power moment)
Inside any project, press **Ask Intelligence** → the assistant scopes itself to *that* project and returns the full intelligence recommended system: verdict, delay outlook, ranked actions, root causes, KPIs, cost of inaction, and cited evidence.

## Verify it worked
- Every action names an owner and a deadline — no orphan advice.
- Click **Track as intervention** → the issue appears in the Interventions Centre at step 1 (Detected).

## Under the hood (honesty)
Recommendations are derived from the same risk register and prediction factors — the "why" always cites the evidence the platform actually has.
